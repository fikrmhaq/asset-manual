Alphabetical. For a shorter, ordered introduction see
[Basic terminology](/getting-started/basic-terminology).

## A

**Active (record)** — a record in current use. The opposite of inactive. See
[Active and inactive](/concepts/active-and-inactive).

**Active (lifecycle state)** — shown as *In use*. A unit currently in service.
Stored as `ACTIVE`. Not the same as an active record.

**Asset** — the master record for a kind of thing. See [Asset](/concepts/asset).

**Asset unit** — one individual physical item. See
[Asset Unit](/concepts/asset-unit).

**Attachment** — a file on an asset. PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX or XLSX, up to 10 MB.

**Attribute** — an extra field configured for a classification, at asset or unit
scope. See [Attributes](/concepts/attributes).

**Audit log** — the automatic, permanent record of who changed what. See
[Audit log](/concepts/audit-log).

## B

**Borrowing** — a loan of one or more units, tracked as a single event. See
[Borrowing](/concepts/borrowing).

**Borrowing item** — one unit's membership of a borrowing. It has no status of
its own.

## C

**Category** — a broad grouping above classifications. Read-only. See
[Category](/concepts/category).

**Classification** — the fixed reference hierarchy assets and locations are filed
under. See [Classification](/concepts/classification).

**Condition** — the physical state of a unit: Good, Fair, Poor, Damaged,
Unserviceable. See [Condition](/concepts/condition).

**Contract** — a procurement agreement. See [Contract](/concepts/contract).

**Contract item** — one purchased line on a contract. See
[Contract item](/concepts/contract-item).

## D

**Department** — a flat, organization-wide grouping saying who is responsible for
a unit. See [Institutions and departments](/concepts/institutions-and-departments).

**Draft** — a borrowing that exists but has not started. Holds no units.

## F

**Floor plan** — an image of a location with its inner locations drawn on. See
[Floor plan](/concepts/floor-plan).

**Floor-plan area** — one shape on a plan, linked to a location inside the one
the plan depicts.

**Funding source** — where the money for a contract came from. A list you
maintain.

## H

**Here only** — the location scope showing units recorded at exactly that
location. See [Viewing what is in a location](/locations/units-in-a-location).

**History** — a unit's timeline of condition, location and lifecycle state. See
[History](/concepts/history).

## I

**Inactive** — a record withdrawn from use but kept, with its history. See
[Active and inactive](/concepts/active-and-inactive).

**Including inside** — the location scope showing units here and in everything
nested within.

**Institution** — an organizational unit that owns records, and the boundary of
what you can see. See
[Institutions and departments](/concepts/institutions-and-departments).

## L

**Lifecycle state** — where a unit is in its working life. See
[Lifecycle state](/concepts/lifecycle-state).

**Location** — a place a unit can be. Locations nest. See
[Location](/concepts/location).

**Lookups** — the screen holding the six short reference lists. See
[Reference lookups](/administration/reference-lookups).

## M

**Measurement unit** — what a Number attribute measures in. A list you maintain.

## O

**Overdue** — a borrowing still holding its units past the expected return date.
Set automatically overnight.

## P

**Permission** — one action on one kind of record, written `resource:action`. See
[Permission reference](/reference/permission-reference).

## R

**Registered** — the lifecycle state of a newly created unit: on the books, not
yet in service.

**Role** — a named set of permissions. Users hold roles. See
[Roles and permissions](/concepts/roles-and-permissions).

## S

**Scope (attribute)** — whether an attribute describes the asset or the unit.
Permanent.

**Scope (organization)** — the institution boundary on what you can see.

**Supplier** — the party a contract is executed with. See
[Vendor vs Supplier](/concepts/vendor-vs-supplier).

**System-wide** — a role setting that lifts the institution boundary. Grants
nothing by itself.

## T

**Transaction** — a permanent record of an event affecting units. See
[Transaction](/concepts/transaction).

**Transaction type** — the kind of event a transaction records. A list you
maintain.

## U

**Unit** — short for asset unit.

**Unserviceable** — a condition value meaning beyond economic repair.

## V

**Vendor** — the manufacturer or brand behind an asset. See
[Vendor vs Supplier](/concepts/vendor-vs-supplier).
