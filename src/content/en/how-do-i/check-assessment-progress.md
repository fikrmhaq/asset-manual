Two places answer this: the **Dashboard**, for the current picture, and the
**reports**, for any month and for exporting.

## On the Dashboard

1. Open the **Dashboard** — it is the first item in the navigation.
2. Find **Monthly assessment coverage**. Each row is a department.
3. Read the **Coverage** column: the bar, the count and the percentage.
4. Click the number under **Not assessed** to open the list of units still
   outstanding for that department and month.

The month picker at the top right of the card changes which month you are
looking at. It opens on the current one.

> [!TIP]
> Work from the **Not assessed** list rather than from the unit list. It is
> already filtered to exactly the units that need you, in your own departments.

## Recording the assessments

From the outstanding list, open a unit and use **Record a change**. Fill in the
condition, the location if it has moved, and a description of what you found.
See [How do I record a change to an asset
unit?](/how-do-i/record-a-change-to-a-unit).

The Dashboard figure updates as soon as you go back to it.

## Seeing who has been doing them

The **Assessment activity** card lists contributors for the month, with two
separate counts:

- **Entries** — how many times they recorded something.
- **Units** — how many distinct units they touched.

Those are different numbers, and only the second relates to coverage. A
contributor who is not the department's person in charge is marked, because
their entries do not count towards that department's total.

## For another month, or to export

Go to **Reports & Audit › Reports** and open **Monthly assessment coverage** or
**Assessment activity**. Both take a month filter and can be exported as CSV.

**Units by location and condition** is there too, if what you need is where the
damaged items are rather than who checked them.

## If a department shows no percentage

- **No person in charge** — nobody is assigned to it. Ask an institution
  administrator to set one; see [Institutions and
  departments](/concepts/institutions-and-departments).
- **No units** — nothing is assigned to that department, so there is nothing to
  assess.
