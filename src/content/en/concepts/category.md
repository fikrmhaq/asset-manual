A **category** is a broad grouping that sits above
[classifications](/concepts/classification). Where a classification might be a
very specific kind of office chair, its category is the wide family that kind of
chair belongs to.

## What you can do with it

Very little, deliberately. Categories are reference information: you can see the
category a classification belongs to when you inspect it under
**Classifications**, and you can view the list under **Lookups → Categories**.

You cannot create, edit or delete them. The Categories tab says so plainly:
categories are maintained outside this application.

> [!NOTE]
> Categories are the only tab under **Lookups** that cannot be edited. The other
> five — measurement units, contract types, funding sources, transaction types
> and account codes — are yours to manage. See
> [Reference lookups](/administration/reference-lookups).

## When you will meet one

In practice, rarely. Assets are filed against a classification, not a category,
and nothing asks you to choose a category. It is context — it tells you which
family of things a classification belongs to when you are inspecting the
hierarchy.
